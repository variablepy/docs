---
title: "Pricing"
description: "How HydroSolve charges tasks across every supported captcha type."
---

HydroSolve reports pricing in two different places. It is important to distinguish between them.

## Per-solve pricing

Every completed solve reports its cost in the `cost` field returned by [`/getResult`](/endpoints/get-result). Prices are in USD.

| Task type | Price per solve |
| --- | --- |
| `HCaptchaCaptchaToken` | `$0.00075` |
| `HCaptchaEnterpriseToken` | `$0.00150` |
| `TurnstileCaptcha` | `$0.00060` |
| `TurnstileCaptchaProxyless` | `$0.00060` |
| `AWSWafToken` | `$0.00100` |
| `AWSWafTokenProxyless` | `$0.00100` |
| `DataDomeTagsCookie` | `$0.00100` |

## Balance deducted when creating a task

When you submit a task to [`/createTask`](/endpoints/create-task), HydroSolve deducts an amount from your balance based on the task shape:

| Condition | Balance deducted |
| --- | --- |
| Base task | `0.5` solve |
| hCaptcha task includes `rqdata` | `1.0` solve |
| Task type contains `enterprise` (`HCaptchaEnterprise`) | `1.0` solve |

<Warning>
  The `cost` field returned by `/getResult` and the balance deducted at `/createTask` are two independent values. Do not treat them as interchangeable.
</Warning>

## Balance conversion

Your balance is stored in solves and reported in USD by [`/getBalance`](/endpoints/get-balance) using:

```text
1 solve = $0.0015
```
