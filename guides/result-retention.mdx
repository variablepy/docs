---
title: "Result Retention"
description: "How long HydroSolve stores task results."
---

Task results are retained for approximately **300 seconds** after the task completes. After the retention window elapses, the result is no longer available from [`/getResult`](/endpoints/get-result).

## Recommendations

- Poll `/getResult/{task_id}` on a short interval (1 to 3 seconds) after submitting a task.
- Persist the token in your own system as soon as you retrieve it. HydroSolve is not a durable store.
- If your workflow can tolerate delay, ensure your polling loop runs within the 300-second window.
